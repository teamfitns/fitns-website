TRAIN WITH PURPOSE

Not every run should feel hard.

Different training intensities create different adaptations. Easy running develops aerobic capacity and allows recovery, while faster sessions target speed, threshold and running economy.

Training pace should also be adjusted according to how you actually feel on the day.
