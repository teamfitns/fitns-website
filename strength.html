THE FITNS.ARC APPROACH

Train with purpose.

Strength training isn't about randomly adding exercises to a workout. It's about having a plan, progressively challenging yourself and giving your body enough time to adapt.

Your training is structured around progressive overload, exercise selection, recovery and measurable progress.

Who is this for?

Beginners

Learn how to train safely and confidently while building a strong foundation.

Muscle & Strength

Follow a structured program designed to progressively build strength and muscle.

Fat Loss

Combine resistance training with nutrition and lifestyle changes to improve body composition.

Returning to Training

Get back into training with a structured, progressive approach instead of starting randomly.

What's included?

Personalised Program

A structured training plan built around your goal, experience and available equipment.

Progressive Overload

Clear progression so you know when and how to increase your training stimulus.

Exercise Guidance

Understand exercise selection, technique, volume, intensity and recovery.

Progress Tracking

Track your lifts, performance and progress so your training evolves with you.
